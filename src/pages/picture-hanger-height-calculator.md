As featured on a Martha Stewart Living show in the early 2000s, this innovative method has stood the test of time and remains the go-to solution for achieving perfect picture placement.

Methodology:

Artwork hung too high or too low can disrupt the space's design aesthetic. Martha Stewart, the homemaking goddess, suggests a foolproof method that the center of the art piece should be at eye level and that the most common eye level is 60 inches. This easy-to-use calculator has you enter the height of the artwork, the distance between the top of the artwork and the hook, and the height you want the center of your artwork to be located (remember Martha Stewart suggests the center of the artwork should be at around 60 inches). The calculator returns the height of where the hook should be located.

Instructions:

Measurements are in inches and eighths of an inch. To work in metric, set Units to Centimeters: each measurement is then one number in centimeters (decimals allowed), anything already entered is converted, and the results are given to the nearest millimeter.

- Enter the height of the art piece as inches and fraction of inches.

- Enter the distance between the top of the artwork and the hook (with the wire or hanger pulled taut) as inches and fraction of inches.

- Enter the height you want the center point of the art to be located as inches and fraction of inches. As described in Martha Stewart Living, the optimal height for the center point of the artwork is 60 inches. You can use this recommended center of artwork height or choose your height.

- The hook height appears in the results, and updates as you type.

There is a secondary calculator where you can enter the artwork's width, and the calculator will provide the centerline dimension and the artwork in thirds.

**Formula:** hook height = center height + picture height ÷ 2 − distance from the top of the picture to the hook.

**Example:** a picture 24 in high, with the wire pulling to 3⅜ in below the top, centered at 60 in: 60 + 24 ÷ 2 − 3⅜ = 68⅝ in.

**Metric example:** a picture 61 cm high, with the wire pulling to 8.5 cm below the top, centered at 152 cm (60 in): 152 + 61 ÷ 2 − 8.5 = 174 cm.
