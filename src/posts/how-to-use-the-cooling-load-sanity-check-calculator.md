This Cooling Load Ballpark Estimator is a valuable tool for architects, engineers, building owners, and developers to use in pre-design meetings and during the design phase as a ballpark estimate for your load calculation.

You can access all our calculators on the HVAC Calculators page.

As a caveat, the calculator is not intended to replace a load calculation. Instead, it gives an insight into what other buildings of the same type have historically installed for refrigeration, occupancy, and lights and equipment. These estimates do not consider standard inputs used in a load calculation, such as building material efficiencies, geographic location, building orientation, and the number of windows. When using this calculator, consider that as buildings, lighting, and equipment become more efficient, the watts and tons will trend lower.

Example:

As with most of our calculators, you can choose US or metric units. Let's demonstrate this calculator using US units. We will evaluate a 10,000 SF (929 m^2) office space. The data for this calculator was gathered from various sources, and the output information may look different depending on the Building Type you select. Choose US units, enter 10,000 SF as the project area, and select Office: One Story as the building type. The result is an estimated 29 tons of cooling. You can play around with the building type to gain more information about the range of refrigeration you might expect. Let's select Office: Office Buildings (General). The results now show a capacity of 28 tons to 53 tons. You can discern whether your building will be on the high or low end of this estimate based on things like orientation, location, and quality of construction.
