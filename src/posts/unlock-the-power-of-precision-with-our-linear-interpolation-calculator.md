## What is Linear Interpolation?

Linear interpolation estimates values that fall on the same line as two known values in a sequence or a dataset. Essentially, it's about finding the "mystery" values based on the assumption that the change between the known values is linear. This technique is widely used in various fields, such as engineering, physics, finance, and data science.

Imagine you have data points representing the temperature at different times of the day. If you need to know the temperature at a time that wasn't recorded, linear interpolation can help you accurately estimate that temperature.
